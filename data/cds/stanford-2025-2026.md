# Stanford CDS 2025-2026 pilot

Official report: [Stanford Institutional Research & Decision Support](https://irds.stanford.edu/data-findings/cds), [2025-2026 PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1GIPKgVj1d86dkmLkHI_mZVCk_iY6kiCp/view?usp=sharing).

The official PDF could not be downloaded in this workspace. Values were transcribed from a [readable copy of the report](https://www.scribd.com/document/1058710035/Stanford-common-data-set); the C1 admissions counts and C9 score ranges were also checked against [an independent transcription](https://www.collegedata.fyi/schools/stanford).

| Site fields | CDS location | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enrollment totals | B1, printed page 4 | Undergraduate 7,346; graduate 10,775; total 18,121. |
| Undergraduate race and ethnicity | B2, printed page 5 | Used the rightmost total undergraduate column; its categories sum to 7,346. |
| Applications, admits, enrolled | C1, printed page 9 | Used reported totals of 60,646, 2,302, and 1,839. Acceptance rate and yield are calculated from these counts. |
| SAT and ACT | C9, printed page 12 | Used reported percentiles and submission rates (55.57% SAT, 20.94% ACT). |
| Tuition, fees, food and housing | G1, printed page 23 | Total of $91,518 is the sum of $67,731, $843, and $22,944. G1 labels these as 2026-2027 costs. |
| Financial aid | H2, printed page 27 | Used full-time undergraduate column: 3,693 of 7,554 for `percentReceivingAid`, $75,816 average aid package, $71,440 average need-based grant, and 100% average need met. H2 reports 2024-2025 final aid figures. |

The C7 admissions factors remain sourced to the prior CDS because the readable text does not preserve the factor table's column positions. The optional in-state/out-of-state residency breakdown was omitted because the report supplies a percentage rather than headcounts.
