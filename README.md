## What is this?

**College Statistics** is a free website for students, parents, and counselors who want real numbers about colleges without paying for a consultant or digging through government databases.

Every figure on the site comes from a school's official **Common Data Set (CDS)**, a standardized report that colleges publish each year covering admissions, test scores, costs, financial aid, and enrollment. The trouble is that those reports are long PDFs scattered across hundreds of university websites. College Statistics gathers them, pulls out the numbers that matter, and turns them into clean pages and charts you can compare side by side.

## What you can explore

| | |
|---|---|
| 📈 **Admissions** | Applications received, acceptance and yield rates, and early decision/action statistics |
| 📝 **Test Scores** | SAT and ACT score distributions, including 25th, 50th, and 75th percentile ranges |
| 💵 **Costs** | Tuition, fees, room and board, and total cost of attendance |
| 🤝 **Financial Aid** | Share of students receiving aid, average aid packages, need-based grants, and net price |
| 🌎 **Demographics** | Enrollment totals, racial and ethnic breakdowns, and where students come from |

Coverage spans **60+ universities** with up to **ten years of history** (2016–17 through 2025–26), so you can see how a school has changed over time and not just where it stands today.

## Features

- **School pages**: a full profile for each college, with trend charts for every category
- **Trends**: compare how a single metric (say, acceptance rate) has moved across many schools
- **University of California & Cal State dashboards**: system-wide views of the UC and CSU campuses
- **My Schools**: sign in to save a college list and keep private notes on each school

## Where the data comes from

Colleges publish the Common Data Set themselves, so the numbers here are the ones the schools report. To turn those PDFs into reliable data, each report goes through a pipeline:

1. **Find & fetch**: locate the official CDS files for each school and year
2. **Extract**: two AI models from different model families read each report and pull out the values separately
3. **Cross-check**: their answers are compared, and any disagreement is resolved by hand against the original page
4. **Validate**: every record is checked against a strict schema before it can be published

If you spot a number that looks off, please [get in touch](https://collegestatistics.org/contact).
